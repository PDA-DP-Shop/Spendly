User Manual

🚦 Step-by-Step App Walkthrough Guide

Follow these screens and setup instructions to learn how to operate the Spendly ecosystem like a pro.

📱 App 1: Spendly User (The Shopper Wallet App)

1

Unlock with Biometrics (Passkey LockScreen)

When you open the app, it prompts you to log in. Click **Unlock** to trigger your phone or PC's built-in Touch ID, Face ID, or PIN window.

Secured by WebAuthn Secure Enclave
2

Count notes in Cash Wallet

Go to the cash counter tab. Instead of just typing a random number, enter the exact count of your physical notes (e.g. ₹500, ₹200, ₹100 notes). The wallet updates the final total balance instantly.

Wallet Balance = (500 * count) + (200 * count)...
3

Parse bank SMS transaction alerts

Copy a transaction SMS from your bank (e.g. *"Rs. 1,500 debited at STAR BAZAR"*), paste it into the parser box, and click **Auto Parse**. The local regex engine extracts the merchant, amount, and category, and logs it directly to your expenses history.

No background system SMS permissions required!
4

Log traditional cash gifts (Shagun gifts)

Go to the Shagun tab. Add logs for cash envelopes given to or received from relatives during weddings and festivals. This tab maintains reciprocal balances for social gifts.

Maintains balance lists: Given vs. Received

🛒 App 2: Spendly Shop (Merchant POS Cash Register)

1

Pair Register with local store Hub

On the Shop register, type in the 6-character connection code displayed on the central store's Radar screen (e.g. `K4N9X3`). Click Connect to establish direct WebRTC data sync channels.

Matchmaking code is validated on Cloudflare Worker KV
2

Checkout & GST/VAT tax calculation

Cashier scans product barcode. Shop queries Radar database over WebRTC, fetches item prices, auto-calculates SGST/CGST tax splits, applies active store discounts, and generates a formatted invoice.

POS query: PRODUCT_REQUEST -> PRODUCT_RESPONSE

📡 App 3: Spendly Radar (Store Manager & Analytics Hub)

1

Configure store stock catalog directories

Store manager adds inventory items to `db.radarStock` database, specifying the barcode reference, item name, retail price, and wholesale cost.

Data stored locally inside IndexedDB via Dexie.js
2

Verify Promotional Claim Codes

When a user claims an offer, the system generates a 6-digit claim PIN. The POS register sends an `OFFER_VALIDATE_REQUEST` to Radar. Radar checks its database, invalidates the code, and confirms the discount.

Automatic real-time campaign coupon check

⛓️ Ecosystem Inter-App Connections (How They Work Together)

1. Shop POS + Radar Hub Sync

Shop POS needs product descriptions and pricing details from Radar's stock records to prepare transactions. When checkout completes, Shop sends a stock update packet to Radar, decrementing inventory counts.

WebRTC Direct Connection

2. Shop POS + User Wallet Billing

Shop POS beams compiled invoice payloads directly to User. Shoppers can tap their phone on the cashier screen (Web NFC Beam), receive it over paired WebRTC connections, or scan the register's dynamic QR code.

NFC, WebRTC, QR Codes

3. User Wallet + Radar Trends

Shoppers discover active campaigns on local Radar hubs. After checkouts, consumer devices geocode coordinates (e.g. Mumbai, Powai), strip user identifiers, and post category/spend totals anonymously to display trend charts.

Privacy Analytics Shards
