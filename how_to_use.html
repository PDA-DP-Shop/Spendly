User Manual

🚦 Step-by-Step App Walkthrough Guide

Follow these screen-by-screen setup instructions to learn how to operate every single feature in the Spendly ecosystem.

📱 Spendly User: Consumer Wallet Manual

Spendly User runs inside the shopper's browser origin sandbox. All data is committed locally using IndexedDB (Dexie.js) to guarantee zero-knowledge privacy.

1. Passkey Login Setup

When you launch the app, you will land on the **LockScreen**.

  • Click the **Unlock** button.
  • The browser will trigger your device's biometrics dialog (Fingerprint/Touch ID/Face ID/Windows Hello).
  • Verify your identity. The Secure Enclave derives a decryption key locally to open your IndexedDB files.

2. Cash Note Counter Wallet

Navigate to the cash wallet page. This tracks physical notes in hand.

  • You will see input fields for ₹500, ₹200, ₹100, and ₹50 notes.
  • Click the **+** or **-** buttons to update note counts.
  • The wallet calculates the total physical cash dynamically.

3. Air Share P2P Receive

Navigate to the Air Share screen to receive invoices directly from the shop register without using any internet server.

  • Click **Receive Bill** on your User app screen.
  • The app opens an encrypted direct WebRTC channel with the paired POS shop register.
  • Confirm the incoming transaction. The invoice payload is automatically imported and saved to your history.

4. Budget Alert Settings

Configure local monthly budget boundaries and payment alerts.

  • Set your monthly spending limit threshold (e.g. ₹10,000).
  • The app calculates daily expenditure patterns and raises alert logs if your costs cross limits.

🛒 Spendly Shop: Point-of-Sale Register Manual

Spendly Shop is an offline cash register used by cashier staff to bill products, calculate taxes, and beam invoices.

1. Connect Register to Radar

When you launch Shop POS, you must pair it with the central store database.

  • Check the Spendly Radar dashboard screen for the 6-character Pairing Code.
  • Type this code in the Shop pairing screen and click **Connect**.
  • The app retrieves connection keys via Cloudflare Worker KV and opens a direct WebRTC tunnel.

2. Scans Product Barcodes

To build an invoice, cashiers scan items directly.

  • Click the **Scan Barcode** camera window.
  • Hold the product barcode in front of the lens.
  • The Shop app sends a `PRODUCT_REQUEST` over WebRTC to Radar and fetches the item description and price.

3. GST Billing, Printed Receipts & WhatsApp Sharing

Review item pricing lines, apply SGST/CGST tax splits, subtract store discounts, and finalize the check.

  • The billing calculator processes CGST and SGST splits automatically.
  • Printed Receipts (D-Mart Style): Click **Print Receipt** to send the layout directly to a connected physical thermal receipt printer.
  • WhatsApp Billing Link: Enter the customer's name and mobile number. The app formats the invoice details and creates a quick link to share the invoice via WhatsApp Web.
  • Air Share: Click **Air Share** to beam the encrypted invoice payload over your peer-to-peer connection.

📡 Spendly Radar: Store Manager Hub Manual

Spendly Radar is run by store owners to manage product stock registries, active promotions, and local sales trends.

1. Manage Inventory Catalogs

Configure store stock logs.

  • Go to the **Stock Directory** tab.
  • Click **Add New Item**.
  • Enter the item name, barcode, retail price, and wholesale purchase cost. Click **Save**.
  • This data is stored inside IndexedDB and is queryable by paired registers over WebRTC.

2. Validate Promotional Claims

Validate shopper coupon claims.

  • Radar generates 6-digit claim PINs when shoppers select local offers on their phones.
  • When the cashier enters the PIN in Shop POS, Radar validates the code, applies the discount, and marks the coupon as used.

3. View Sales Trend Analytics

Track sales intelligence metrics.

  • Go to the **Analytics Dashboard** tab.
  • Analyze local store performance via Recharts trend lines.
  • View geocoded user data, stripped of all personal names and identifiers, to track sales by category and amount anonymously.

🔌 P2P Synchronization Matrix

Spendly's sync pathways are split across three network boundaries to enable offline operation:

1. Shop POS + Radar Hub Sync

Shop POS needs product descriptions and pricing details from Radar's stock records to prepare transactions. When checkout completes, Shop sends a stock update packet to Radar, decrementing inventory counts.

2. Shop POS + User Wallet Billing Sync

Shop POS beams compiled invoice payloads directly to User. Shoppers receive invoices over direct WebRTC connections via Air Share, WhatsApp messaging redirect links, or physical printed paper bills.

3. User Wallet + Radar Trends Sync

Shoppers discover active campaigns on local Radar hubs. After checkouts, consumer devices geocode coordinates (e.g. Mumbai, Powai), strip user identifiers, and post category/spend totals anonymously to display trend charts.

🚀 Step-by-Step Ecosystem Walkthrough Guide

1

Configure stock in Radar

Open Spendly Radar, go to **Stock Directory**, and add catalog items (e.g. barcode "890103", item name "Soda", price "50", wholesale cost "30").

2

Get the Pairing Code

Radar generates a temporary 6-character connection code (e.g. `K4N9X3`) and posts it to the Cloudflare Worker API.

3

Pair POS Shop register

Open Spendly Shop register, enter the code `K4N9X3`, and click Connect. The register queries Cloudflare Workers KV and establishes a direct WebRTC connection with Radar.

4

Discover promotions on local Radar

Open Spendly User. The app discovers active campaign lists on the local Radar hub.

5

Claim discount coupon

Click "Claim" on an offer. Radar registers the request and returns a unique 6-digit claim PIN.

6

Scan item barcodes during checkout

Cashier scans item barcode. Shop POS queries Radar database over WebRTC and fetches item details (`PRODUCT_REQUEST` -> `PRODUCT_RESPONSE`).

7

Redeem discount coupon

Cashier inputs the shopper's 6-digit claim PIN. Shop POS verifies the code with Radar over WebRTC (`OFFER_VALIDATE_REQUEST`). Radar confirms the code and applies the discount.

8

Update stock levels

Shop POS sends a stock update packet to Radar, decrementing stock levels for the checked-out items.

9

Invoice sharing and printing

Shop POS register prints out a formatted receipt page like D-Mart, generates a share link to send via WhatsApp, or transmits the bill over Air Share.

10

Save invoice

The shopper imports the invoice into their local expenses database.

11

Log store credit (Credit flow fallback)

If checking out on credit, the transaction is logged to the merchant's local ledger and updated in the customer's personal credit tracking folder.

12

Process local area analytics

User app geolocates coordinates locally (e.g. Mumbai, Powai), strips all personal names or identifiers, and posts transaction totals anonymously to display local Recharts sales trend graphs on Radar.
