"Thank you, Meet. Hello Mam, I am Rudra. I will show you how our P2P connection channels operate under the hood.

👩‍🏫 Mam's Expected Viva Question Bank

Q1. Why are you calling it a zero-knowledge app?

Because there is no central database server. All data stays locally inside the browser's IndexedDB origin sandbox on the user's device.

Q2. What is IndexedDB and why did you not use simple LocalStorage?

LocalStorage is limited to 5MB and runs synchronously, blocking the main thread. IndexedDB has no strict storage limits and queries asynchronously, so it doesn't freeze the screen.

Q3. If there are no servers, how do devices exchange data?

They communicate directly using WebRTC DataChannels (PeerJS wrapper), with handshake signaling brokered via public WebSocket topics on ntfy.sh.
